## Worked example (a real decision the platform makes)

- **Title:** Lane DE-07 — transport drift triggers safety-stock recalibration
- **Trigger:** Transport lead time observed at 5 days actual vs 7 days planned,
  sustained over 14 shipments.
- **Decision:** Reduce safety stock on Lane DE-07 — recalibrated 620 → 480 units
  per SKU across 14 SKUs.
- **Guardrail result:** Service level maintained at 98.5%.
- **Impact facts:** Working capital +$142K · Service impact None · SKUs affected 14 ·
  Target system SAP ECC
- **Governance:** Awaiting human approval → Accept & writeback → persisted to SAP ECC.
- **Speed:** Total cycle 36 seconds, then the network returns to watching.

### The closed loop (steps)
1. Observation — lead time has improved on the lane.
2. Signal down — the new lead-time pattern feeds Dynamic Safety Stock.
3. Recalibrate — safety stock can drop; working capital release computed.
4. Validate downstream — confirm no shortage exposure across DCs.
5. Orchestrate — the change is validated and a decision bundle compiled.
6. Surface to human — a decision card is posted to the team; agents pause.
7. Approved — a human accepts.
8. Writeback — parameters persisted to the ERP.
9. Loop closed — value released; the network resumes watching.
